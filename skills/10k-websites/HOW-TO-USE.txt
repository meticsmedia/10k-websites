10K WEBSITES SKILL FOR CHATGPT

Install it once:
1. Open the ChatGPT desktop app and switch to Codex (top-left menu).
2. Drag this zip file into a chat and send:  Install this skill
3. Wait for ChatGPT to confirm it is installed.

Build a website:
1. Create a new empty folder for your website and open it in Codex.
2. Start a new chat, type /  and pick 10k-websites. Send it. No other instructions needed.

ChatGPT will check your setup, ask you a few questions about your business,
offer Focused budget, Studio, or Full throttle production, design your site,
create its imagery and opening film, and put it live on a free Hostinger address or your domain.
Full throttle uses the top available GPT throughout and the strongest suitable
image/video models with high-quality settings and room for refinement.
You choose the direction and approve the visuals and spending; the AI handles the work.
The skill stays installed, so you can do this again for every new website.

Paused or ran out of usage? Return to the same chat when access is available and
say continue. If a new chat is needed, open it in the same folder and invoke
10k-websites again; it resumes from the saved plan. New chats do not reset usage limits.
